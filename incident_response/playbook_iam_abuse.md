# Incident Response Playbook

## Scenario

Privilege Escalation

## Detection

AdministratorAccess policy attached to a user.

## Investigation

1. Determine who attached the policy.
2. Review recent activity.
3. Identify affected resources.

## Containment

1. Remove elevated permissions.
2. Disable compromised accounts.

## Recovery

1. Restore least privilege.
2. Review IAM policies.
